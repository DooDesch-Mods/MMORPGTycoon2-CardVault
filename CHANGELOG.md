# Changelog

## 0.1.0

### Added

- Browse and search community character cards inside MMORPG Tycoon 2, with artwork previews and class, monster and boss filters.
- Import cards into your character library from the game or send them directly from the Card Vault website.
- Export the character you have open, check its preview and publish it to Card Vault.
- Connect your game through Discord in your browser. Disconnect at any time from the mod.
- Open Card Vault from Mods... and keep readable card names in the game's Prefabs list.
